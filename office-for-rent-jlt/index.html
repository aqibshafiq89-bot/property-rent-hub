Working from JLT

Jumeirah Lakes Towers is a mixed-use community of office and residential towers built around artificial lakes, with Dubai Marina on the other side of Sheikh Zayed Road. The towers are grouped into clusters identified by letters, so most JLT addresses include a cluster letter as well as the tower name.

The community's master developer is DMCC (Dubai Multi Commodities Centre), which is also the free zone authority that licenses companies here. DMCC's headquarters is in Almas Tower, the tallest building in JLT.

Getting there

Two Red Line stations serve JLT: DMCC station, which used to be called Jumeirah Lakes Towers, and Sobha Realty station, formerly Dubai Marina. Both connect with the Dubai Tram. Cafés and restaurants at lake level make it easy to meet clients without leaving the community.

Who it suits

Companies licensed by DMCC, including trading firms, startups and international businesses, and teams who live in Dubai Marina or JLT and want a short commute.

Licence and Ejari

JLT is inside the DMCC free zone, and DMCC sets its own workspace and lease rules for the companies it licenses. If you hold, or plan to get, a mainland licence from the Department of Economy and Tourism (DET), ask before you commit. DMCC offers a Dual Licence Office Permit that allows a DMCC company's DET-licensed branch to operate from the same JLT office, but other arrangements depend on the building. We will check what applies to the office you are considering.

What to check before renting in JLT

  • Licence fit. Confirm the office can be used with your licence, whether DMCC or mainland.
  • Lease registration. Ask whether the lease is registered with DMCC, through Ejari, or both.
  • Cluster and tower. Check the walk from the tower to DMCC or Sobha Realty station and to the tram.
  • Parking. Ask how many bays come with the office and where visitors can park.
  • What the rent covers. Confirm what is included, such as furniture, internet, utilities and meeting rooms.

Questions about offices in JLT

More answers on our FAQ page.

Is JLT a free zone?

Yes. JLT is part of the DMCC free zone. DMCC is both the community's master developer and the authority that licenses companies there.

Can a mainland company rent an office in JLT?

In some cases. DMCC offers a Dual Licence Office Permit that allows a DMCC company's DET-licensed branch to operate from the same JLT office. Other arrangements depend on the building, so ask us about the specific office.

Which metro stations serve JLT?

DMCC station and Sobha Realty station on the Red Line. Both connect with the Dubai Tram.
