Choose your type of workspace

Not sure which you need? Our guide to private offices and shared workspaces explains the difference.

  • Furnished private office

    A lockable office for your team with the furniture already in place, so you can move in and start working. Ask whether internet, utilities and meeting-room access are included in the rent.

    Ask about furnished private offices

  • Serviced office

    A private office inside a managed business centre, with shared services such as reception and meeting rooms. Services are usually bundled with the rent; confirm exactly what is covered.

    Ask about serviced offices

  • Shared workspace

    A desk in a shared office. It keeps costs down for freelancers and one- or two-person companies. Check that the arrangement is accepted for your licence before you sign.

    Ask about shared workspaces

  • Virtual office

    A business address without a desk of your own. Some licence types accept this and others do not, so confirm with your licensing authority first and ask us what each package includes.

    Ask about virtual offices
