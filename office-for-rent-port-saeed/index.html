Working from Port Saeed

Port Saeed is a neighbourhood in Deira, one of Dubai's oldest districts, on the northern bank of Dubai Creek. Offices, hotels and residential towers share the area. Deira City Centre mall sits at its heart, Dubai Creek Golf & Yacht Club takes up much of the land by the water, and Dubai International Airport lies just to the east.

Getting there

City Centre Deira station on the Red Line is in the middle of the district, and Deira City Centre bus station is close by. From here the Red Line runs south through Sheikh Zayed Road and Business Bay to Al Barsha and JLT, which helps when your team lives in different parts of the city.

Neighbouring areas include Al Garhoud, Al Muraqqabat and Al Khabisi. If your clients drive in from across the creek, look at the route they will take at peak times before you choose a building.

Who it suits

Deira has been a trading district for generations, and Port Saeed suits import and export firms, wholesalers and other trading companies, along with startups and small teams looking for an affordable office in a central location. Being close to the airport also helps if you travel often or receive regular visitors.

Licence and Ejari

Port Saeed is part of mainland Dubai, so offices here are generally used with trade licences from the Department of Economy and Tourism (DET), with the lease registered through Ejari. Ejari availability depends on the office, so ask us to confirm it for the one you like before you commit.

What to check before renting in Port Saeed

  • The building's condition. Deira has buildings from many different decades. During the viewing, look at the lifts, air conditioning, washrooms and common areas.
  • Parking and access. Ask about allocated or nearby paid parking, and how visitors, couriers and deliveries reach the office.
  • Size and layout. Count the desks and storage you need, including space for samples or files if you trade goods.
  • What the rent covers, and VAT. Ask what is included and whether the 5% VAT on commercial rent is on top.
  • Ejari and permitted use. Check that the office can be registered for your licence activity.
