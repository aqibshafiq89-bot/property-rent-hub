Our objective is to simplify the office search process by providing clear property information, convenient communication and personalised viewing assistance.

We focus on understanding our customers' business requirements, preferred locations and rental budgets before helping them explore appropriate workspace options.

How we help

  1. We start with your requirements

    Area, team size, budget, move-in date and whether you need Ejari for your trade licence.

  2. We share offices that match

    Each with its rental details, what the rent includes and its Ejari information, so you can compare like with like.

  3. We arrange the viewing

    You see the office in person and we stay in touch on WhatsApp until you have decided.

Where we work

We advertise offices in five Dubai areas: Port Saeed, Sheikh Zayed Road, Business Bay, Al Barsha and Jumeirah Lakes Towers. We arrange viewings at each property; we do not run a branch in every area.

How we keep information accurate

  • We share current rental details for each office on request and confirm them in writing before any agreement.
  • When we show photos of a specific office, they are photos of that office. General pictures on this website are marked as illustrations.
  • We do not publish reviews or ratings on this website.
  • Our guides explain rules in plain language and link to official sources. They are general information, not legal advice.
