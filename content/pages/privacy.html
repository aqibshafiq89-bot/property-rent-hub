Information you send us

When you contact us on WhatsApp or by phone, we receive your phone number, your name if you share it, and the details in your message, such as the area, team size, budget and move-in date you are looking for. We use this only to reply to you, suggest suitable offices and arrange viewings.

If you use the office enquiry form on this website, the details you type (such as your name, phone number, email, company and office requirements) are placed into a WhatsApp message on your own device. Nothing is sent until you press Send in WhatsApp, and this website does not store the form.

WhatsApp is operated by Meta. Messages you send through it are also covered by WhatsApp's own privacy policy.

Website analytics

If analytics is switched on for this website, we use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site: pages viewed, the type of device and browser, approximate location and which buttons are clicked, including the WhatsApp and call buttons, and when an enquiry form is sent, with the location, team size and office type chosen. Analytics never receives your name, phone number or email. We use this to improve the website. Google Analytics uses cookies; you can block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

Sharing

We do not sell your information. We share your details with a landlord or property manager only when that is needed to arrange a viewing or a tenancy you have asked about.

Keeping and deleting information

We keep enquiry details for as long as they are useful for your search and our records. To ask what we hold about you, or to ask us to delete it, message us on WhatsApp or call us using the details below.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the bottom of this page shows when it last changed.
