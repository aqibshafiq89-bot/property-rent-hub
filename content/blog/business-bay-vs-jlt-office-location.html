Business Bay and Jumeirah Lakes Towers (JLT) are both busy tower districts on the Dubai Metro Red Line, and both attract startups and established companies. The difference that matters most is not the view or the commute. It is licensing.

The short answer

  • If your company holds, or will hold, a mainland licence from Dubai's Department of Economy and Tourism (DET), Business Bay is the straightforward choice.
  • If your company is, or will be, licensed by DMCC, JLT is the natural home, because it sits inside the DMCC free zone.

Once licensing is settled, compare location, commute and setting.

Licensing

Business Bay

Business Bay is part of mainland Dubai. Offices there are generally used with DET trade licences, and the office lease is registered through Ejari, the Dubai Land Department's tenancy registration system. Ejari registration is a normal part of issuing and renewing a mainland licence, so confirm it is available for the specific office.

JLT

JLT is part of the DMCC free zone. DMCC, a free zone authority set up by the Dubai government in 2002, is both the master developer of the JLT community and the authority that licenses companies there. It sets its own workspace and lease rules for the companies it licenses.

Mainland companies are not automatically excluded from JLT, but the options are narrower. DMCC offers a Dual Licence Office Permit that allows a DMCC-licensed company's DET-licensed branch to operate from the same JLT office. Other arrangements depend on the building, so ask before you commit.

Location and commute

Business Bay is central. It lies just south of Downtown Dubai along the Dubai Water Canal, between Sheikh Zayed Road and Al Khail Road, with DIFC a short drive away. Business Bay station serves the district, and Burj Khalifa/Dubai Mall, Financial Centre and Emirates Towers stations are close to parts of it.

JLT is in the west of the city, beside Dubai Marina on the other side of Sheikh Zayed Road. It has two Red Line stations, DMCC (formerly Jumeirah Lakes Towers) and Sobha Realty (formerly Dubai Marina), and both connect with the Dubai Tram.

A good test is to plot where your team lives and where you meet clients. If most journeys point towards Downtown, DIFC or Deira, Business Bay will save time. If your team lives around Dubai Marina, JLT, Jumeirah Village or Discovery Gardens, JLT usually means shorter commutes.

Setting and day-to-day life

Business Bay is a district of office and residential towers along the canal, with hotels and restaurants close to Downtown Dubai. It covers a large area, so the walk from a particular tower to the metro can be short or long. Check it before you sign, especially if staff will commute by train in the summer.

JLT is built around artificial lakes, with towers grouped into lettered clusters and cafés and restaurants at lake level. Many people both live and work there, which gives it a neighbourhood feel during the day and in the evening.

Office costs

Rents in both districts vary widely with the tower, floor, view, size and fit-out, so we quote them on request rather than as a single figure. When you compare offers, use the full yearly cost: rent, 5% VAT, lease registration, the security deposit, service or chiller charges, utilities and parking.

Which should you choose?

  • Choose Business Bay if you hold a mainland licence, meet clients around Downtown and DIFC, or want a central address that is easy to reach from both ends of the city.
  • Choose JLT if you are licensed by DMCC, your team lives in west Dubai, or you want a community where your staff can eat, meet and work in one place.
