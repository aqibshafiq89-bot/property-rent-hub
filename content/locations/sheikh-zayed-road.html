Working on Sheikh Zayed Road

Sheikh Zayed Road (E11) is Dubai's main highway, running from the Trade Centre area past Business Bay, Al Barsha and Dubai Marina towards Abu Dhabi. Office and hotel towers line long stretches of it, and an address on the road is easy for clients and couriers to find.

Because the road is so long, "Sheikh Zayed Road" can describe very different locations. A tower near the World Trade Centre and one near Al Barsha are a fair distance apart, so always look at the exact building on a map.

Getting there

The Dubai Metro Red Line runs alongside the road. World Trade Centre, Emirates Towers and Financial Centre stations serve the northern stretch near DIFC and the Museum of the Future, and the line continues past Burj Khalifa/Dubai Mall and Business Bay towards Mall of the Emirates and JLT.

Who it suits

Companies that meet clients often, want a recognisable corporate address, or need quick road access towards Abu Dhabi and the northern emirates. It also suits regional offices and established businesses that want to be seen on Dubai's main road.

Licence and Ejari

Licensing depends on the building. Many towers on the road are in mainland Dubai, where a Department of Economy and Tourism (DET) licence uses an Ejari-registered lease. Nearby districts such as DIFC are free zones with their own licensing and lease rules. Tell us which licence you hold or plan to get, and we will only suggest offices that fit it.

What to check before renting on Sheikh Zayed Road

  • Which side of the road the building is on. Reaching the opposite carriageway can mean a detour, so plan how clients will arrive by car.
  • The walk to the metro. Check the distance to the nearest station and whether there is a footbridge or covered route.
  • Parking. Ask how many bays come with the office and where visitors can park.
  • Licensing authority. Confirm whether the building is mainland or in a free zone, and whether Ejari is available.
  • What the rent covers. Ask what is included, such as furniture, internet, utilities and meeting rooms.
