Working from Al Barsha

Al Barsha sits in west Dubai between Sheikh Zayed Road (E11) and Sheikh Mohammed Bin Zayed Road (E311), just south of Al Sufouh. It is divided into Al Barsha 1, 2 and 3 and Al Barsha South. Al Barsha 1 is the busiest part: it runs alongside Sheikh Zayed Road and is home to Mall of the Emirates, with hotels, restaurants and apartment buildings around it.

For an office, the draw is access. Dubai Marina, Dubai Internet City and Al Quoz are a short drive away, and two metro stations sit on the main road.

Getting there

Mall of the Emirates and InsuranceMarket stations on the Red Line both serve Al Barsha 1. Mall of the Emirates station connects to the mall through an enclosed bridge. InsuranceMarket station was previously called Sharaf DG and then Mashreq, and Dubai Metro stations are renamed from time to time, so check the current name in the RTA app or Google Maps before you send directions to visitors.

By car, Sheikh Zayed Road, Al Khail Road and Umm Suqeim Road all lead into the area.

Who it suits

Al Barsha works well for consultants, small service companies and growing teams that want a west Dubai address with straightforward road and metro access. It is also practical if your staff commute on the Red Line, which links Al Barsha with Business Bay, Sheikh Zayed Road, Port Saeed and JLT.

Licence and Ejari

Al Barsha is not a free zone, so offices here are generally used with mainland trade licences issued by Dubai's Department of Economy and Tourism (DET). A mainland licence relies on an office lease registered through Ejari, the Dubai Land Department's tenancy registration system. What to check before renting in Al Barsha

  • The exact address. Listings sometimes group Barsha Heights (TECOM) with Al Barsha. It is a separate community, so confirm the building and where it is.
  • The walk to the metro. A few minutes on foot is easy in January and hard work in August.
  • Parking. Ask whether spaces come with the office and where visitors can park.
  • What the rent covers. Furniture, internet, electricity and water, cleaning, reception and meeting rooms vary from office to office.
  • Ejari. Confirm the office can be registered for your business activity.
