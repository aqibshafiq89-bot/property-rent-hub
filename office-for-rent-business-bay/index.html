Working from Business Bay

Business Bay is a district of office and residential towers just south of Downtown Dubai, set along the Dubai Water Canal. It lies between Sheikh Zayed Road (E11) and Al Khail Road (E44), so it is easy to reach by car from most of the city, and DIFC is a short drive away.

Getting there

Business Bay station on the Red Line serves the district, and Burj Khalifa/Dubai Mall, Financial Centre and Emirates Towers stations are nearby. Business Bay covers a large area, though, and some towers are a long walk from the nearest station, which matters in summer. Check the route before you choose a building.

Who it suits

Professional service firms, consultancies, agencies and established companies that want a central address close to Downtown Dubai and DIFC. Options range from compact furnished offices to larger fitted spaces.

Licence and Ejari

Business Bay is part of mainland Dubai, so offices here are generally used with Department of Economy and Tourism (DET) licences and leases registered through Ejari. We confirm Ejari availability for each office before you commit.

What to check before renting in Business Bay

  • The walk to the metro. Some towers are close to a station and some are not.
  • The office's condition. Offices are let furnished, fitted (partitions, ceilings and air conditioning in place) or as shell and core (bare space). Each needs a different budget and timeline.
  • Parking and visitor access. Ask how many bays come with the office and how visitors get in.
  • What the rent covers. Ask what is included: furniture, internet, utilities, cleaning and meeting rooms vary from office to office.
  • Ejari. Confirm the office can be registered for your activity.
