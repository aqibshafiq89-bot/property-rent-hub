How Much Does It Cost to Rent an Office in Dubai?

By the Property Rent Hub teamUpdated 22 November 20263 min read

There is no standard rent for an office in Dubai. Two offices of the same size can rent for very different amounts, depending on where they are, how they are fitted out and what the contract includes. This guide explains what decides the rent, and how to get rental details that match what your business needs.

1. Location

The area matters, and so do the building, the floor and the view. Offices a short walk from a metro station, or with easy access from a main road, are usually in higher demand. Best Areas to Rent an Office in Dubai compares five business districts.

2. Size and layout

Start from the number of people who will work in the office, then add what else it must hold: a meeting table, storage, a reception desk or samples. Some listings give a size that includes a share of the building's common areas, so ask for the usable area as well.

3. Furnished, fitted or shell and core

A furnished office is ready to use on the day you move in. A fitted office has partitions, ceilings and air conditioning but no furniture. A shell-and-core unit is bare space that needs a full fit-out, usually with approval from the building management. Furnished vs. Unfurnished Offices in Dubai compares the options.

4. What the rent includes

Some offices include internet, electricity and water, cleaning, reception and meeting rooms in the rent; others include none of them. Ask for a written list of what is included with each office, so you can compare them fairly.

5. Contract terms

The length of the contract, the move-in date and the payment schedule all shape the final terms. Tell us what suits your business and we will look for offices that match.

6. Parking and access

Ask how many parking bays come with the office and where visitors can park. If your team drives, check the route at rush hour before you decide.
