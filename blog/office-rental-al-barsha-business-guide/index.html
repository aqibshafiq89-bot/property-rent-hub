Office Rental in Al Barsha: A Business Guide

By the Property Rent Hub teamUpdated 22 November 20264 min read

Al Barsha is one of the easier parts of Dubai to explain to a client: "near Mall of the Emirates" is usually enough. That familiarity, plus quick access to Sheikh Zayed Road and the metro, is why many small companies look here for an office. This guide covers what you need to know before you rent one.

Where Al Barsha is

Al Barsha is in west Dubai, south of Al Sufouh, bounded by Sheikh Zayed Road (E11) on one side and Sheikh Mohammed Bin Zayed Road (E311) on the other. It is divided into Al Barsha 1, 2 and 3 and Al Barsha South.

Al Barsha 1 is the commercial heart. It runs along Sheikh Zayed Road and is home to Mall of the Emirates, with hotels, restaurants, apartment buildings and office buildings around it. Al Barsha 2 and 3 lie further inland and are largely residential.

One thing to watch when you search: listings sometimes group Barsha Heights (also known as TECOM) with Al Barsha. It is a separate community, so check the exact building before you arrange a viewing.

Getting to an office in Al Barsha

By metro

Two Red Line stations sit on Sheikh Zayed Road beside Al Barsha 1:

  • Mall of the Emirates, connected to the mall by an enclosed bridge.
  • InsuranceMarket, between Mall of the Emirates and Dubai Internet City stations. It was called Sharaf DG until 2020 and Mashreq until 2024.

Station names change from time to time under the RTA's naming-rights programme, so check the current name before you put it in directions for clients or new staff. The Red Line connects Al Barsha with JLT and Dubai Marina in one direction, and with Business Bay, the Sheikh Zayed Road towers and Deira in the other.

By car

Sheikh Zayed Road, Al Khail Road and Umm Suqeim Road all reach the area. If clients will drive to you, check which side of Sheikh Zayed Road the building is on and how they get into its car park, and ask about visitor parking.

Who Al Barsha suits

  • Consultants and service businesses that want a recognisable west Dubai address without being in a high-rise tower district.
  • Small teams whose staff live in west Dubai or commute on the Red Line.
  • Companies working with clients in Dubai Marina, Dubai Internet City, Dubai Media City and Al Quoz, all a short drive away.

It is less of a fit if your clients are mostly in Deira or near the airport; Port Saeed is closer for them.

Licensing and Ejari in Al Barsha

Al Barsha is not a free zone. Companies based there generally hold mainland trade licences from Dubai's Department of Economy and Tourism (DET), and a mainland licence needs an office lease registered through Ejari, the Dubai Land Department's tenancy registration system.

In practice, that means two questions before you sign:

  1. Can this office be registered through Ejari in your company's name?
  2. Is the office suitable for your licence activity?

Viewing checklist for Al Barsha

  • Confirm the community (Al Barsha 1, 2, 3 or Al Barsha South) and the exact building.
  • Walk the route from the office to the nearest metro station. In summer, a short distance on the map can feel long.
  • Ask how many parking spaces come with the office and what visitors should do.
  • Check what the rent covers: furniture, internet, electricity and water, cleaning, meeting rooms and reception.
  • Get written confirmation that Ejari is available for your company and activity.
  • Look at building access hours if your team works early, late or at weekends.
