Some office rental options may include Ejari. Confirm availability and eligibility for the specific property before signing an agreement.

Our guide What Is Ejari and Why Is It Important for Office Rentals? explains how registration works.

Can I rent an office for my new company in Dubai?

Office requirements depend on your business activity, licensing authority and applicable regulations. Contact us to discuss suitable workspace options.

The office rental checklist for new businesses sets out the steps in order.

What documents do I need to rent an office?

Landlords usually ask for your trade licence (or your initial approval if the company is still being set up) and passport and Emirates ID copies for the person signing. Registering the lease through Ejari also needs the company's licence details. Requirements vary by landlord and licensing authority, so we confirm them for each office.
